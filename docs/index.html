How does this work?

This tool analyzes final blows from zKillboard data to find who kills a character most often. Your nemesis is the single pilot with the most final blows against you.

Data covers the last 365 days and updates weekly. Only characters with 3+ losses in that period are tracked.

The reverse nemesis section shows pilots who you kill the most — meaning you are their nemesis.
